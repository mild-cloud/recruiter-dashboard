💡 Actions to Improve Conversion
Intro → Offer · 17% · fix this first
Before each intro: confirm the JD, budget, and hiring timeline with the client — don't send profiles into a vacuum.
After each intro: set a hard feedback deadline with the client (5 business days). Chase on day 6.
Snowflake (12 intros, 0 placements): pause new intros until you have a confirmed open headcount.
ICI → Re-ICI · 33% · book it before you hang up
End every ICI call by booking the Re-ICI on the spot — don't follow up cold.
For non-P1 candidates who go quiet: send one short message with a specific role attached, not a generic check-in.
Track ghosting by source — if certain outreach channels have lower Re-ICI rates, deprioritise them.
P1 Quality · tighten the bar at ICI stage
Before marking someone P1: ask — do I have a live role I'd submit this person for today?
Review your non-P1 intros: if they're rarely converting, stop spending Re-ICI time on them and redirect to sourcing better-fit ICIs.
Target: push P1 share from 35% toward 45% of ICIs.
Offer → Placement · 67% · close the 6 dropoffs
Pre-close call: before the offer lands, call the candidate — confirm they're still in and nothing has changed.
Set expectations early: cover comp range, start date, and contract type at Re-ICI stage, not offer stage.
Counter-offer prep: ask directly — "if your current employer matches, what would you do?" — and address it before it happens.
